# Feature register

This register preserves planned scope as well as bounded implemented subsets. It is a backlog, not a declaration of production readiness. IDs are stable requirement references, not PR counts. Refinements may split rows but must retain their parent mapping in the reconciliation record. No deletion or downgrade of scope is implied by generic wording.

Milestones derive from historical planning and are dependency stages, not dates. Related entries overlap intentionally; do not sum them as independent features. Installation-specific requirements remain outside source control. See [scope](SCOPE.md), [MVP acceptance](MVP_ACCEPTANCE.md), [dependencies and reconciliation](SCOPE_RECONCILIATION.md), and [baseline evidence](VALIDATION.md). The pre-review baseline contains 149 references: the original 135 (99 named entries and 36 cross-cutting bundles), 12 configurable-installation requirements and two task/model research requirements approved on 8 November 2026. The current register has 155 references: that baseline plus six approved deferred supporting bundles (REV-01–06). These are requirement references, not independent deliverables. Original 99 product-feature counts remain separate.

For a quick counted projection, see the [feature overview](FEATURE_OVERVIEW.md) and [downloadable dashboard](FEATURE_DASHBOARD.html). [The critical review](FEATURE_REVIEW.md) covers each reference and shared implementation boundaries; the review alone does not change scope. The owner has now approved its full improvement programme and A–F additions, deferred until after the existing scope; see [the approved contract](DEFERRED_REVIEW_PROGRAMME.md). Refresh its counters and version mapping after verified feature merges or approved scope changes; do not count an open PR as merged or a partial foundation as a complete feature.

## Status and acceptance policy

Partial = an implemented bounded subset with remaining work. Planned = full acceptance not evidenced; inspect before claiming absent or complete. Every row stays open until its complete specification, negative permission tests, provenance/export/recovery behavior, required exact-head CI and relevant real-host/provider gates are satisfied. No row here is certified production complete.

Before implementing a row, add measurable acceptance to its feature spec and preserve all applicable constitution/security constraints. Keep data local, unknowns explicit, originals/provenance intact, approvals payload-bound and side effects authorized. A title or generated suggestion never grants authority.

Referenced ranges summarize related contracts rather than granting all their capabilities to a row.
