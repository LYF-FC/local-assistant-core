## Later contract safeguards

These refine register rows without claiming implementation:

- Household: units/batches/location and accountable consumption/transfer/correction ledger; ordered goods require receipt review. Link equipment to invoices/warranties/serials/manuals; controlled manufacturer fetch verifies type/model; cleanup previews retain rollback manifests. Exports are views, not concurrent authoritative stock stores.
- Care: restricted observations with timestamp/source and explicit unknown states; preserve parent/clinician-approved instructions and product-specific opening/shelf-life rules. Nutrition and allergens use dated guidance; trends are not diagnosis. Health-provider and phone export routes require feasibility/consent; fitness reminders do not assume a provider API.
- Education: version curriculum system/stage/domain, normalized concept/mapping, regional language expression, licensed resource and private observation records. Configurable primary curricula take priority over comparative systems; use official authorities and dated revisions. French/Spanish/English vocabulary is distinct from concept understanding. No ranking/deficit inference or forced completion percentages. Parent-managed interests, stage, materials and school/move continuity guide gentle recommendations; mappings, sources, decisions and corrections remain auditable.
- NOMAD: separate hardware and independent direct offline access. Constrained health/catalogue/search/resource/Kiwix/Kolibri capabilities require verified endpoints, private authentication, bounded responses and attribution. No unrestricted finance/health/mail/database/credential transfer or automatic reverse bridge. Optional capability-scoped shared local inference exposes no general memory and is unnecessary for appliance availability.
- Professional/creative: separate grants, roles/namespaces, documents/keys, credentials/indexes, prompts/cache/history, jobs/audit/export and retention/recovery. Deny household-to-work and work-to-household retrieval. Keep unknown owner/date explicit in debriefs; distinguish observations, suggestions, confirmed decisions and completed actions. Real accounts require source/employer policy review. Use commercially compatible voice licenses; non-commercial speech is not a business fallback. Media/project annotations and feedback are opt-in; validated corrections update only the correct project/client/general style scope. Verify actual editor APIs or offer reviewable interchange/select lists. Reopen projects with linked media; preserve originals and versions; final creative approval does not authorize sending/publication.
- Finance: reconcile inputs before surplus/trajectory calculations. Version assumptions, dates, currencies, taxes/jurisdictions, scenarios and decision evidence; recompute numerical claims deterministically. Manual property/opportunity research is on-demand until a cadence is explicitly configured. Review packs support redacted professional summaries and private details; no lender contact/application, transfers or trading without separate authority, with autonomous financial execution excluded.
- R&D/Discovery: service inventory tracks installed/available/stable/approved versions, advisories/support/EOL, licenses, compatibility and exception expiry. Benchmarks and migration/restore/rollback proposals are sourced and bounded. Product Discovery adds correctable preferences, rejection/snooze/topic suppression and safe checkpointed collectors. Separate private/professional photo evaluation with rights/license/quality review; never scan real media from expressed interest alone.
- Mail authentication: approved local aggregate reports only; bounded XML/archive parsing, deduplication and normalized reporter/domain/period/IP/count/SPF/DKIM/alignment with provenance. Administrator-scoped history/export/retention; reviewed authorized sources and calibrated quiet anomalies. No mailbox source, recurring collection or DNS changes activated by planning.
- External AI: evaluate local baselines first; provider controls are unresolved until verified for the actual endpoint/account. Classify/minimize deterministically, fail closed on uncertainty; approval binds exact content hash, provider/model, purpose, policy, expiry, retention and budget. Reserve bounded budgets and enforce one allowlisted egress path/global disable; no invisible rerouting or unlimited retries. Credentials/raw memory exports and protected categories remain blocked. Responses are untrusted advice, numerical claims are recomputed locally and tools still require local authority. Local operation survives denial/outage.
