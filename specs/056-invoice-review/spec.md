# 056 — Invoice field review and duplicate hints

## Specify

A member opens an existing reviewed document, prepares bounded deterministic invoice-field candidates, inspects source-line/PDF-page evidence and OCR uncertainty, explicitly confirms or leaves each field unknown, corrects that review, sees possible visible duplicates and exports/history or forgets the structured copy. DOC-02/MVP-03 advance without mail accounts, model inference, filing, ledger/payment state or private deployment activation. All 149 scope IDs remain.

## Clarify / checklist

- Existing shared/creator-private document scopes only. Shared members read current eligible invoice reviews and duplicate hints; creator alone prepares/reviews/history/exports/forgets. Private requires owner plus current grant; admin is not content authority. Source document identity/scope/owner never changes.
- Reuse current reviewed source text with successful PDF/OCR extraction. One invoice resource/document; pin source revision/original and text hashes. Replacement, re-extraction, review withdrawal or any document revision invalidates the current structured view. A stale/unreviewed/forgotten source never releases old derived fields through current/shared reads or duplicate matching; source history/derived history remain creator-only. Reprepare is explicit and resets field attestation.
- Six fields: issuer, invoice number, issue date, due date, total, currency. A fixed EN/FR/ES label vocabulary scans at most 1,000 logical lines, at most 256 Unicode characters/1 KiB per evidence line, preserving complete exact lines and page intersections. At most three candidates/field; disclose truncation/missing/conflicting/unsupported formats. No arbitrary PDF/parser/network/model execution. Candidates are suggestions, not independently verified facts or numerical confidence scores; reviewed OCR remains uncertain.
- Dates use unambiguous ISO dates; amounts are signed decimal strings with exactly two digits, no exponents/group separators/floats, up to 999,999,999.99 in magnitude. A single decimal comma can normalize to a dot only under that grammar. Supported explicit currencies: EUR/USD/GBP/CHF/CAD; currency symbols and other formats stay unsupported/unknown until human correction. No implied paid/unpaid, taxes, exchange rates, totals arithmetic or banking authority. Due date cannot precede issue date when both confirmed.
- Human review explicitly attests the original and all six decisions: confirmed valid value or unknown/null. Confirmed fields either cite a bounded current line (server reconstructs exact evidence) or require a manual-original-check note. Corrections can differ from candidate text and remain explicitly human attested; no automatic truth claim. Unknown values are not inferred zero/negative/absent. A reviewed resource may retain unknown fields; matching needs confirmed issuer and invoice number.
- Duplicate hints are read-only, one chosen scope/current visibility, at most 25 UUID-keyset matches/page: exact original checksum, or current reviewed issuer/number normalized only by case/whitespace. Never merge/delete/suppress imports, infer across private scopes, expose hidden counts, treat same number/different issuer as equal, or certify business identity. Stale or unreviewed field reviews cannot supply key matches. Equal keys/different content remain possible conflicts for human review.
- User/session/membership → source document → invoice locks through metadata audit commit. Expected document/review revisions prevent lost updates; same-source draft prepare can replay as a no-op after lost acknowledgement. Recheck expiry after waits/read construction. Duplicate reads lock selected current documents/reviews; no mutation/retry or publication.
- At most 100 invoice revisions plus terminal forget; history/export five revisions/page and acknowledged unmanaged-copy limits. Prepare reuses source quotas and bounded evidence; no duplicate original/index store. Forgetting the invoice scrubs current/all derived history/keys but retains the original document, and is terminal for that resource. Forgetting the document atomically scrubs invoice current/history/keys too, including at limits. No audit/query content, invoice values, quotes, invoice-key hashes or source text in logs. Old backups/downloads remain outside recall.
- UI lives in document detail, clears when the selected document/revision/scope/view/session changes, treats source text as inert, retains conflicts until reload, and never retries mutations or uses browser storage. Source/field review and duplicate hints remain distinct from filing approval/action.

## Acceptance

1. EN/FR/ES labeled synthetic text, missing/conflicting/unsupported dates/amounts/currencies, scan bounds and PDF/OCR exact-line/page/hash provenance; malicious instructions are inert.
2. Prepare/manual review/correction/unknowns/history/export/terminal forgetting; source drift/review withdrawal/forget cannot release stale derived values or duplicate keys.
3. Shared/private/same-grant/admin/revocation/session/CSRF/origin negatives; after-wait expiry, optimistic/concurrent updates, audit failure rollback and aggregate document-forget rollback.
4. Exact/key duplicate hints, conflict preservation, no cross-scope/owner leakage, current-source requirements and pagination.
5. Actual restricted PostgreSQL guards/grants/migration round trip, concurrency/role/audit proof and complete logical restore with current/forgotten derived history.
6. Trusted-HTTPS/mobile document-to-review workflow, unknown values/duplicates/history/export, drift and late-view clearing. Container fixture uses actual migration-owner authority and CI default butler, with runtime refusal before privileged seed.
7. Required checks/privacy/security/149-ID reconciliation, one coherent commit/PR/push; report expected full CI and stop without polling/auto-merge.
