## Findings made while implementing (fixed or stated)

- nginx serves `.mjs` as `application/octet-stream` with `nosniff`, so a `?url` pdf.js module worker was blocked; the worker is now bundled as a classic same-origin script (`?worker`) and the CSP is untouched.
- The first scan fixture was 1.3 MB (noise defeats compression) against the 128 KiB original limit; it is now 22 KB at 100 dpi without noise. On this setup OCR dropped the blurred Total line (status still `ready`) and read the invoice number with a space (`MVP 01-…`); both are recorded as observed once and never asserted.
